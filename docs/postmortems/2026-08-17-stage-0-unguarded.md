# A run convened with the wrong departments passed every check

- **Date:** 2026-08-17
- **Severity:** High. The most serious design finding in the project.
- **Found by:** A deliberate sabotage test, run as part of the stress suite
- **Status:** Documented, not fixed. No fix exists in the current design.

## The experiment

The same question was put through two Quick runs.

One with a fit cabinet: Money and Law, The Funders, Options Beyond Cutting.

One deliberately mis-convened: **Digital Presence and Brand, Staff Wellbeing and Culture, Premises and Facilities**, for a question about allocating £50,000 between two programmes under a funding gap.

## What happened

Every department in the sabotaged run produced a competent, well-sourced, genuinely self-critical report. Each one traced its claims to primary sources. Each one rejected marketing material correctly, naming the commercial interest. Each one identified its own weakest assumption and said so.

**Not one noticed the cabinet was wrong for the question.**

Then neither checking layer caught it.

The **auditor** verifies claims within a report. Three accurate reports pass, and these were accurate.

The **cross-checker** hunts contradictions between reports. Three irrelevant reports do not contradict each other. It found real clashes on sequencing and messaging, all of them internal to a mis-framed question, and reported them faithfully.

The mismatch surfaced only at the branches, after every pound of research had been spent. The Comptroller's ruling: the check on cabinet formation **"lives nowhere structural"**, it "lived in one node's discretion at the end".

## What did work

Both Speakers behaved correctly. The sabotaged run's Speaker refused to answer, ruled the run incapable of addressing the question, and issued an interim ruling only.

That refusal is the system working. It is also the most expensive possible place to discover the problem.

## Root cause

Stage 0 is the highest-leverage decision in a run and the only one with nothing checking it. One agent reads the question and names the departments. Everything downstream is shaped by that choice, and every downstream check is scoped to verify work *within* the frame rather than to question the frame.

A departmental self-attack stays inside its own remit. It asks whether its position is right, never whether its remit belongs.

## Why it is not fixed

Adding a Stage 0 auditor was considered and rejected during the self-scrutiny run, on the grounds that a same-model reviewer would ratify the cabinet and launder the choice rather than check it. That objection has not been answered.

The honest position is that the check needs to be a human one, or a genuinely different model, and neither is currently in the design.

## Lesson

**A run is won or lost at Stage 0, and nothing downstream can rescue it.** Every check in Quorum verifies work against a frame. None of them checks the frame.

Documented in `SKILL.md` and the README as a known limitation rather than quietly carried.
